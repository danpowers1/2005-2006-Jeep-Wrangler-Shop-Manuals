DRIVEABILITY - NGC

Symptom:
    P0562-BATTERY VOLTAGE LOW



When Monitored and Set Condition:

        P0562-BATTERY VOLTAGE LOW
        When Monitored:      The engine running. The engine speed greater than 380 RPM.
        Set Condition:    Battery voltage is 1 volt less than desired system voltage.



                                         POSSIBLE CAUSES
GOOD TRIP EQUAL TO ZERO
BATTERY POSITIVE CIRCUIT HIGH RESISTANCE
GENERATOR GROUND HIGH RESISTANCE
GENERATOR OPERATION
(K125) GEN FIELD CONTROL CIRCUIT OPEN
(K125) GEN FIELD CONTROL CIRCUIT SHORTED TO GROUND
(Z20) GROUND CIRCUIT OPEN
