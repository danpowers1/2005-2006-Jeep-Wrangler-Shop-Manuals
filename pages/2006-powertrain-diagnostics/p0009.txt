                                                                     GENERAL INFORMATION
NOTE:
The Powertrain Control Module and Transmission Control Module are combined into a single
module. This module is referred to as the Next Generation Controller (NGC). Two tools are
required to diagnose and repair the PCM terminals and harness connectors:
1. Miller #3638 Terminal Removal Pick must be used to release the connector terminals or harness and
   connector damage will occur.
2. Miller #8815 Pinout Box must be used to probe the PCM terminals or terminal damage will occur.
