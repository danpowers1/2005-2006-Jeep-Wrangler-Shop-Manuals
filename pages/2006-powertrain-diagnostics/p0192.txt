DRIVEABILITY - NGC

Symptom:
    P0508-IAC VALVE SIGNAL CIRCUIT LOW



When Monitored and Set Condition:

        P0508-IAC VALVE SIGNAL CIRCUIT LOW
        When Monitored:       Engine running. Battery voltage greater than 10 volts. IAC motor
        operating.
        Set Condition: The PCM senses a short to ground or battery voltage on the Linear Idle Air
        Control (LIAC) control circuit for 2.75 seconds while the IAC motor is active.

(K61) IAC CONTROL CIRCUIT SHORTED TO GROUND
(K961) IAC SIGNAL CIRCUIT OPEN

(K61) IAC CONTROL CIRCUIT OPEN
