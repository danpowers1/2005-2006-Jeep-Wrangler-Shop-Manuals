                                                                          DRIVEABILITY - NGC

Symptom:
    P0580-SPEED CONTROL SWITCH NO.1 LOW



When Monitored and Set Condition:

        P0580-SPEED CONTROL SWITCH NO.1 LOW
        When Monitored:       With the ignition key on. Battery voltage above 10 volts.
        Set Condition:    When switch voltage is less than 0.60 of a volt for 2 minutes.



                                           POSSIBLE CAUSES
SPEED CONTROL SWITCH VOLTAGE LOW
SPEED CONTROL ON/OFF SWITCH
SPEED CONTROL RESUME/ACCEL SWITCH
CLOCKSPRING
(V37) S/C SIGNAL CIRCUIT SHORTED TO THE (K900) SENSOR GROUND CIRCUIT
(V37) S/C SIGNAL CIRCUIT SHORTED TO GROUND
PCM
