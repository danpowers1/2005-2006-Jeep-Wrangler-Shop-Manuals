DRIVEABILITY - NGC

Symptom:
    P0452-NVLD PRESSURE SWITCH STUCK CLOSED



When Monitored and Set Condition:

        P0452-NVLD PRESSURE SWITCH STUCK CLOSED
        When Monitored:        Immediately after the engine has been started.
        Set Condition: The PCM activates the NLVD Solenoid. If PCM does not see NVLD switch
        open an error is detected. One Trip Fault.
