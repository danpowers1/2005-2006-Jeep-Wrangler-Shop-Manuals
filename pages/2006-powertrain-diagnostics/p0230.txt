DRIVEABILITY - NGC

Symptom:
    P0594-SPEED CONTROL SERVO POWER CIRCUIT



When Monitored and Set Condition:

        P0594-SPEED CONTROL SERVO POWER CIRCUIT
        When Monitored: Engine running, the speed control switched on and learned, and the
        brake pedal is not pressed. No other fault has occured this trip.
        Set Condition: The speed control power supply circuit is either open or shorted to ground.
        Cruise will be disabled for the remainder of that key-on cycle. One Trip Fault. Three good
        trips to clear the MIL.



                                             POSSIBLE CAUSES
GOOD TRIP EQUAL TO ZERO
(V32) S/C SUPPLY CIRCUIT OPEN
(V32) S/C SUPPLY CIRCUIT SHORTED TO GROUND
BRAKE LAMP SWITCH
(V30) S/C BRAKE SWITCH OUTPUT
(V30) S/C BRAKE SWITCH OUPUT CIRCUIT OPEN
(V30) S/C BRAKE SWITCH OUTPUT CIRCUIT SHORTED TO GROUND
PCM

 TEST                                          ACTION                                             APPLICABILITY

          Is the Good Trip Counter equal to zero?
                   Yes   → Go To     2

                   Yes   → Go To     3
