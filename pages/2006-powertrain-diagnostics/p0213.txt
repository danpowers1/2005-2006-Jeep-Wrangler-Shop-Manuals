                                                                              DRIVEABILITY - NGC

Symptom:
    P0563-BATTERY VOLTAGE HIGH



When Monitored and Set Condition:

        P0563-BATTERY VOLTAGE HIGH
        When Monitored: With the ignition on. Engine RPM greater than 1000 RPM. With no
        other charging system codes set.
        Set Condition: The battery sensed voltage is 1 volt above the charging goal for 13.47
        seconds. The PCM senses the battery voltage turns off the field driver and senses the
        battery voltage again. If the voltages are the same, the code is set.



                                             POSSIBLE CAUSES
GOOD TRIP EQUAL TO ZERO
GENERATOR OPERATION
(K125) GEN FIELD CONTROL CIRCUIT SHORTED TO BATTERY VOLTAGE
PCM
