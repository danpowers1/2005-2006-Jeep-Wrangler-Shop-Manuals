    P0688-ASD RELAY SENSE CIRCUIT LOW

        P0688-ASD RELAY SENSE CIRCUIT LOW
        When Monitored:         With ignition key on. Battery voltage greater than 10 volts.
        Set Condition: No voltage sensed at the PCM when the ASD relay is energized for 3.075
        seconds. Three good trips to clear the MIL.
