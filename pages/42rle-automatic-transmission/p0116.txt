  The manual valve (4) (Fig. 263) is operated by the
mechanical shift linkage. Its primary responsibility is
to send line pressure to the appropriate hydraulic cir-
cuits and solenoids. The valve has three operating
ranges or positions.

CONVERTER CLUTCH SWITCH VALVE
  The main responsibility of the converter clutch
switch valve (10) (Fig. 264) is to control hydraulic
pressure applied to the front (off) side of the con-
verter clutch piston. Line pressure from the regulator
valve (5) is fed to the torque converter regulator
valve (8). The pressure is then directed to the con-
verter clutch switch valve (10) and to the front side
of the converter clutch piston. This pressure pushes
the piston back and disengages the converter clutch.
