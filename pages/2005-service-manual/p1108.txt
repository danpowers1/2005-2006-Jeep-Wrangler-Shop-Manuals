   The PCM uses the MAP sensor input to aid in cal-
culating the following:
   • Manifold pressure
   • Barometric pressure
   • Engine load
   • Injector pulse-width
   • Spark-advance programs
   • Shift-point strategies (certain automatic trans-
missions only)
   • Idle speed
   • Decel fuel shutoff
