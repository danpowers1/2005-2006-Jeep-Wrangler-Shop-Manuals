OPERATION - 4.0L
   The CMP sensor (1) (Fig. 12) uses a rotating hall
effect device called a target wheel (3) which is
attached to the oil pump drive shaft. The target
wheel contains sets of machined notches (2).
   When the leading edge of a target wheel notch
passes by the tip of the CMP sensor, the interruption
of magnetic field causes the voltage to switch high
resulting in a sync signal of approximately 5 volts.
   When the trailing edge of the target wheel notch
passes by the tip of the CMP sensor, the change of
the magnetic field causes the sync signal voltage to
switch low to 0 volts.
