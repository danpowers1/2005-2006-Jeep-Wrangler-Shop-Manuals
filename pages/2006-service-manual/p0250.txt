Towing Requirements — Trailer Brakes
  − Do not interconnect the hydraulic brake system
or vacuum system of your vehicle with that of the
trailer. This could cause inadequate braking and pos-
sible personal injury.
                                                                           Fig. 2 4 - Pin Connector
  − An electronically actuated trailer brake control-
ler is required when towing a trailer with electroni-
cally actuated brakes. When towing a trailer
equipped with a hydraulic surge actuated brake sys-
tem, an electronic brake controller is not required.
  − Trailer brakes are recommended for trailers over
1,000 lbs (454 kg) and required for trailers in excess
of 2,000 lbs (907 kg).
