DRIVEABILITY - NGC

Symptom:
    P2503-CHARGING SYSTEM OUTPUT LOW



When Monitored and Set Condition:

        P2503-CHARGING SYSTEM OUTPUT LOW
        When Monitored:      The engine running. The engine speed greater than 1157 RPM.
        Set Condition: The battery sensed voltage is 1 volt below the charging goal for 13.47
        seconds. The PCM senses the battery voltage turns off the field driver and senses the
        battery voltage again. If the voltages are the same, the code is set.



                                        POSSIBLE CAUSES
GOOD TRIP EQUAL TO ZERO
BATTERY POSITIVE CIRCUIT HIGH RESISTANCE
CASE GROUND HIGH RESISTANCE
GENERATOR OPERATION
(Z20) GEN GROUND CIRCUIT OPEN
(K125) GEN FIELD CONTROL CIRCUIT SHORTED TO BATTERY VOLTAGE
(K125) GEN FIELD CONTROL CIRCUIT OPEN
(K125) GEN FIELD CONTROL CIRCUIT SHORTED TO GROUND
