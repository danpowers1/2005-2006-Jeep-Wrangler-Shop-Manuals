DRIVEABILITY - NGC

Symptom:
    P0112-INTAKE AIR TEMPERATURE SENSOR CIRCUIT LOW



When Monitored and Set Condition:

        P0112-INTAKE AIR TEMPERATURE SENSOR CIRCUIT LOW
        When Monitored:       With the ignition on. Battery voltage greater than 10 volts.
        Set Condition: The Intake Air Temperature (IAT) sensor circuit voltage at the PCM goes
        below 0.5 of a volt. One Trip Failure.



                                           POSSIBLE CAUSES
IAT SENSOR VOLTAGE BELOW 0.5 OF A VOLT
IAT SENSOR
(K21) IAT SIGNAL CIRCUIT SHORTED TO GROUND
(K21) IAT SIGNAL CIRCUIT SHORTED TO THE (K900) SENSOR GROUND CIRCUIT
PCM

 TEST                                        ACTION                                           APPLICABILITY
   1      Ignition on, engine not running.                                                         All
          With the DRBIIIt, read the IAT Sensor voltage.
          Is the voltage below 0.5 of a volt?
                  Yes   → Go To     2

                  No    → Refer to the INTERMITTENT CONDITION Symptom (Diagnos-
                          tic Procedure).
                          Perform POWERTRAIN VERIFICATION TEST VER - 5.

   2      Turn the ignition off.                                                                   All
          Disconnect the IAT harness connector.
          Ignition on, engine not running.
          With the DRBIIIt, read the IAT Sensor voltage.
          Is the voltage above 1.0 volt?

                  Yes   → Replace the IAT Sensor.
                          Perform POWERTRAIN VERIFICATION TEST VER - 5.
                  No    → Go To    3

   3      Turn the ignition off.                                                                   All
          Disconnect the C2 PCM harness connector.
          Measure the resistance between ground and the (K21) IAT Signal circuit at the IAT
          Sensor harness connector.
          Is the resistance below 100 ohms?

                  Yes   → Repair the short to ground in the (K21) IAT Signal circuit.
                          Perform POWERTRAIN VERIFICATION TEST VER - 5.

                  No    → Go To    4
