                                                                            DRIVEABILITY - NGC

Symptom:
    P0107-MAP SENSOR CIRCUIT LOW



When Monitored and Set Condition:

        P0107-MAP SENSOR CIRCUIT LOW
        When Monitored:       Engine speed greater than 250 RPM. Battery voltage greater than 10.3
        volts.
        Set Condition:    The MAP sensor signal voltage is below 0.08 of a volt for 1.7 seconds. One
        Trip Fault.



                                           POSSIBLE CAUSES
MAP SENSOR VOLTAGE BELOW 0.08 OF A VOLT
(F856) 5-VOLT SUPPLY CIRCUIT OPEN
(F856) 5-VOLT SUPPLY CIRCUIT SHORTED TO GROUND
MAP SENSOR
(K1) MAP SIGNAL CIRCUIT SHORTED TO GROUND
(K1) MAP SIGNAL CIRCUIT SHORTED TO THE (K900) SENSOR GROUND CIRCUIT
PCM
