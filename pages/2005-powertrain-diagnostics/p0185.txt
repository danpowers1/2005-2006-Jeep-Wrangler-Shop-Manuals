    P0498-NVLD CANISTER VENT VALVE SOLENOID CIRCUIT LOW



When Monitored and Set Condition:

        P0498-NVLD CANISTER VENT VALVE SOLENOID CIRCUIT LOW
        When Monitored:       Engine running.
        Set Condition:     The PCM detects a short in the NVLD Canister vent solenoid circuits. One
        trip Fault.



                                           POSSIBLE CAUSES
GOOD TRIP EQUAL TO ZERO
NVLD SOLENOID
(K106) NVLD SOL CONTROL CIRCUIT SHORTED TO GROUND
