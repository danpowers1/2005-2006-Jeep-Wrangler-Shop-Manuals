                                                                            DRIVEABILITY - NGC

Symptom:
    P1115-GENERAL TEMPERATURE SENSOR PERFORMANCE



When Monitored and Set Condition:

        P1115-GENERAL TEMPERATURE SENSOR PERFORMANCE
        When Monitored:       With the ignition on and engine running.
        Set Condition: The PCM compares the ouputs of the ECT, IAT, and Ambient Temp
        sensors. If one sensor does not corralate with the other two sensors the fault is set.



                                           POSSIBLE CAUSES
GOOD TRIP EQUAL TO ZERO
TEMPERATURE SENSOR CIRCUT
FAULTY SENSOR
EXCESSIVE RESISTANCE IN THE SENSOR SIGNAL CIRCUIT
EXCESSIVE RESISTANCE IN THE (K900) SENSOR GROUND CIRCUIT
