                                                                          DRIVEABILITY - NGC

Symptom:
    P0499-NVLD CANISTER VENT VALVE SOLENOID CIRCUIT HIGH



When Monitored and Set Condition:

        P0499-NVLD CANISTER VENT VALVE SOLENOID CIRCUIT HIGH
        When Monitored:       Engine running.
        Set Condition: The PCM detects an open in the NVLD Canister vent solenoid circuits.
        One trip Fault.



                                           POSSIBLE CAUSES
GOOD TRIP EQUAL TO ZERO
NVLD SOLENOID
(K106) NVLD SOL CONTROL CIRCUIT SHORTED TO BATTERY VOLTAGE
(K106) NVLD SOL CONTROL CIRCUIT OPEN
(Z913) GROUND CIRCUIT OPEN
PCM
